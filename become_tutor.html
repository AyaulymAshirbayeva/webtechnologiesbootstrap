REQUIREMENTS

Who can become a TilUP tutor?

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Have B2–C2 proficiency or be a native speaker.
  • Be able to communicate clearly in English or another teaching language.
  • Have a stable internet connection and a webcam.
  • Enjoy teaching and helping people learn languages.
  • Teaching certificates or experience are welcome but optional.
